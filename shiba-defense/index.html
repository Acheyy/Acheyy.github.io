Shiba Defense

A free, cozy yokai tower defense for iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

Every autumn, during the Moon-Viewing Festival, the mischievous yokai of the mountain sneak down to raid the village Mochi Storehouse. Not out of malice: moon mochi simply smells wonderful. Only the village dogs stand in their way.

Crew festival stalls, lantern poles, wells and firework tubes with shibas, akitas and other Japanese dog breeds, and keep the raiders away from the mochi. Portrait, one thumb, cozy and never scary.

Two ways to defend

  • Village Campaign: swing the wooden gates to steer each raiding party down the lane where your towers wait.
  • Lantern Garden: lay the stepping-stone path the yokai must follow, piece by piece.

What's in the festival

  • 12 hand-made maps in 3 chapters, from the autumn village to the lantern-lit mountain pass.
  • Six dog crews with two upgrade forks each, and Kuri, a red shiba hero on a festival kite.
  • Tanuki, kamaitachi, oni, keukegen, mirror yokai, crow tengu and kitsune, plus three boss yokai who never lose, they just give up.
  • Three stars per map, Heroic challenges, Casual or Normal difficulty, and free rewind to any wave.
  • A new daily challenge every day, free for everyone.

Free, all of it

Shiba Defense is free: every chapter, every Lantern Garden and the daily challenge. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, no loot boxes, no energy timers and no account, and the game works offline. New maps open as you win the ones before them.

Help and privacy

Questions or a bug to report: see Support. Shiba Defense collects no personal data: read the privacy policy.
