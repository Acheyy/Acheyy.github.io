Support

Contact

Email barbu.bogdan195@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within a few days. For a bug, please include your iPhone model, the iOS version, the game version (shown on the game's menu) and what happened just before.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every chapter of the Village Campaign, every Lantern Garden and the daily challenge are free. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and you never need an account.

How do I open chapter 2, chapter 3 or the next garden?

Win the map before it: each map opens when you win the one before, and a map's card tells you which one that is. A garden opens when you win act 2 of the garden before it. Heroic opens on a map once you have won it.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to another iPhone?

Progress is saved automatically on your iPhone. It is not stored online, so deleting the game deletes your progress. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup includes it, so restoring a backup onto a new iPhone brings it along.

The game restarted when I came back to it

When other apps need memory, iOS may close the game in the background. Shiba Defense picks up again from your saved progress, and offers to resume a battle you were in the middle of. If it happens often during play, please tell us your iPhone model.

Does it work offline? Does it need an account?

It works fully offline and needs no account.

Which devices are supported?

iPhones with iOS 15 or later. On an iPad it runs in iPhone mode. The game plays in portrait with one thumb.

There is no sound

Check the ring/silent switch (the game follows it, so your own music can keep playing) and the sound settings in the game's Settings.

What is the diagnostics panel?

Five quick taps on the version line open a panel of technical details (frame rate, the save's status, recent errors). It stays on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. If we ask for it to help with a bug, you can take a screenshot of it and email it to us.

Does Shiba Defense collect my data?

No. It has no ads, analytics or tracking, and your progress stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
