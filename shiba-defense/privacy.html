Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026. This policy covers the Shiba Defense app for iPhone and this website.

In short

  • Shiba Defense collects no personal data. We have no servers and receive nothing from the game.
  • The game is free: no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone.
  • No analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.

Who we are

Shiba Defense is made by Bogdan Barbu ("we"), an independent developer. For anything about privacy, write to barbu.bogdan195@gmail.com.

What the game stores, and where

The game saves, in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • your progress: maps and garden acts won, stars, best results, daily challenge scores, what you have unlocked and the Workshop upgrades you chose;
  • your settings (music and sound volume, haptics, reduced motion, left-handed controls, starting speed) and which tutorial tips you have seen;
  • simple play counters, such as battles played and won;
  • a backup copy of the save from your last launch, so a damaged save can be recovered, and, if a save ever can't be read, a copy of the damaged one;
  • while a battle is in progress, a record of it, so you can pick it up if the game is closed;
  • if iOS closes the game's page to free memory, how many times that happened, so the game can pick up where you left off and use a lighter picture size if it keeps happening.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which Apple and you control. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game makes no network connections of its own: everything it needs, pictures and sounds included, is inside the app, and it works offline. It is built with the Phaser game engine and the Capacitor app framework, and uses Capacitor's plugins for saving, haptics and noticing when the app goes to the background, and the open-source Capacitor Community Keep Awake plugin to keep the screen on while you play; none of them collects or sends data. The game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social code, and it does not track you across apps or websites. You will never see a tracking permission prompt from Shiba Defense.

The diagnostics panel

Five quick taps on the version line in the game open a diagnostics panel. It shows technical details on your screen: the frame rate and picture size, whether the save loaded cleanly, the sound engine's state, how many times iOS closed the game and the last few error messages. It is put together on your iPhone while the panel is open and is never sent anywhere: the game has no way to send it. If you want help with a problem, you can choose to take a screenshot of it and email it to us; we then treat it like the rest of your email (below).

No purchases, no ads

Shiba Defense is free. It has no in-app purchases and no ads, so the game never handles payment details or shows anyone's advertising. Downloading it from the App Store is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Links

The game's Settings have Support and Privacy buttons. Tapping one opens this website in Safari, outside the game; the game itself sends nothing.

Information Apple may share with developers

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated, anonymous usage statistics (for example, how many people installed the game). We use them only to fix problems and understand the game's reach. They do not identify you.

Emails to support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object or ask us to restrict it at any time. We do not share it (our email provider stores it for us) and delete the conversation when it is resolved (within 12 months at the latest).

Children

Shiba Defense is rated for ages 9 and up and is not directed to children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There is nothing to buy in the game.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about players, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. If you emailed us, you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete that correspondence, to restrict how we use it, or object to our using it. If you are in the European Union or the UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages use no cookies, no analytics and no third-party content. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under its own privacy statement (GitHub General Privacy Statement).

Changes

If a future version of the game starts collecting any data, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will change with it. The date at the top shows the latest version.
