Privacy policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Corgi Blast (the "app") on iPhone. The app is made by Bogdan Barbu ("we", "us"). If anything here is unclear, email us at barbu.bogdan195@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone. We don't run servers, analytics or crash reporting of our own, so we don't receive your game data.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. The app only shows rewarded video ads, and only when you tap a button to watch one. Google may collect some device and usage data to show and measure those ads. It uses your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Optional in-app purchases are paid through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The app keeps a record of what you bought only in the saved game on your iPhone.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game on your iPhone so that you can carry on where you left off. This includes your coins, gems, stars, stage, upgrades, pets, Star Pass progress, settings (sound, music, haptics and so on), a few timestamps, such as when you last played (for offline earnings) and when your next Free Gift is ready, and a record of your in-app purchases (see section 4).

  • This data is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Like other app data, it is part of your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you use them. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.
  • You can delete it at any time: use Settings > Reset progress in the game, or delete the app.

2. Information we collect

We don't collect personal information through the app. We have no user accounts, no backend servers, and no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete the conversation when it's no longer needed.

3. Advertising by Google AdMob

The app is free because of optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch a short video for a ×2 boost, to double your offline earnings, or to double a Free Gift. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). 4. In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases: gem packs, a one-time Starter Pack and the premium track of each Star Pass season. Everything else in the game, gems included, can also be earned by playing. Purchases work like this:

  • Apple processes every payment. You buy through Apple's own App Store purchase sheet, and Apple charges your Apple Account under Apple's terms and its privacy policy. We never receive your payment card or bank details, your name, your email or your Apple Account.
  • What the app receives from Apple, on your iPhone: a transaction signed by Apple with the product you bought, a transaction ID, the purchase date, and whether it was refunded, plus the country of your App Store (to tell you when purchases aren't offered in your region). The app checks the transaction on the device with Apple's StoreKit and then delivers what you bought.
  • What the app keeps: the transaction IDs it has delivered, the Star Pass seasons you own and how many gems you have bought, stored in the saved game on your iPhone (section 1), so that nothing is delivered twice and your purchases can be restored. This record is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app has no server that receives it.
  • Restore purchases (in Settings, the Shop and the Star Pass) asks Apple which purchases your Apple Account owns, again only between your iPhone and Apple.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We don't see your refund requests.
  • Google AdMob is not involved in purchases and receives no purchase data from the app.

Apple keeps its own records of your purchases. You can see them in your Apple Account's purchase history.

5. Your choices

Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

On first launch, iOS may ask whether Corgi Blast can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier isn't shared with Google and the game works exactly the same. You can change your answer at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested, so that you can accept or refuse personalized ads and the related data use. You can review or change your choice later in the game under Settings > Privacy options. This option appears only where such consent applies.

US state privacy rights

Some US states give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. In Corgi Blast, you can do this by not allowing tracking (see above), and through Settings > Privacy options where it is shown. We don't sell personal information ourselves.

You never have to watch an ad or buy anything

Ads are always optional. If you never tap a watch button, the app never shows you an ad. Purchases are optional too. You can block or limit them on your iPhone with Screen Time (Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases > In-app Purchases), and Family Sharing's Ask to Buy lets a parent approve each purchase.

6. Children

Corgi Blast is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no user profiles and no way to share content with other players. If you believe a child has given us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • Game progress and the purchase record: kept on your device until you reset it (Settings > Reset progress) or delete the app. Gems you bought are part of the saved game, so they are deleted with it. The Starter Pack and Star Pass seasons can be brought back with Restore purchases.
  • Purchase and payment records: held by Apple under its own privacy policy.
  • Emails to us: kept only as long as needed to help you.
  • Advertising data: held by Google under its own retention policies. See the Google links above.

8. Security and international transfers

Your game data never leaves your device through us. Google's SDK sends ad requests over encrypted connections, and Google may process data in the United States and other countries under its own safeguards.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold any personal information from the app, there is usually nothing for us to hand over. Requests about advertising data are handled by Google (see the links above). You're welcome to contact us with any question, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add a new service, a cloud save or analytics, we'll update this page and the effective date above before that version of the app is released.
