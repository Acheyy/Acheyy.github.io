Saving and progress

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Just open the game and play.

How is my progress saved?

Automatically, on your iPhone. The game saves every few seconds, after every purchase, hatch and boss win, and whenever you leave the app. Nothing is uploaded, so the game also works offline.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

There is no cloud save or account sync. Your progress is part of your iPhone's normal backup, so restoring an iCloud or computer backup, or transferring your data to a new iPhone during setup, usually brings it along. It does not sync between two devices.

I deleted the app. Can I get my progress back?

Unfortunately not. Deleting the app deletes its saved game, including gems you bought, and we don't keep a copy. The only way back is an iPhone backup made before you deleted it. The Starter Pack and Star Pass seasons you bought come back with Restore purchases (see Purchases below).

How do I start over?

Open Settings (the gear button), tap Reset progress, and confirm twice ("Are you sure?", then "Tap again to wipe ALL"). This deletes everything (coins, stages, gems, pets, upgrades and settings) and can't be undone.

Playing

How do offline earnings work?

When you're away for at least a minute, your corgi keeps earning coins. You get up to 2 hours of earnings at 50% of your normal income. The Cozy Bed gem upgrade raises both the time limit and the rate, and the Star Owl pet adds to it. When you come back, tap COLLECT, or ×2 WATCH to double the coins with a short video ad.

Changing your iPhone's clock won't earn you more: big clock jumps are ignored.

What happens when a boss escapes?

The game keeps you farming the stage's normal waves so you can upgrade. Tap FIGHT BOSS when you're ready. With Auto boss on (Settings), the game retries the boss by itself after each farming loop.

What is Rebirth?

From stage 25 you can rebirth: you start again at stage 1, but you earn stars that permanently increase your damage and income (+10% each), plus gems. Your gems, pets, gem upgrades and stars are kept.

How do pet eggs work? What are the odds?

A Pet Egg costs 100 gems (or 10 eggs for 900 gems, with at least one Epic or better). Gems are earned by playing (bosses, gold asteroids, rebirths, the Free Gift and the Star Pass), and they can also be bought as an optional in-app purchase. Eggs from the Starter Pack and the Star Pass wait in the Shop and hatch with the same odds.

Every egg hatches one of 8 pets. Pets of the same rarity are equally likely:

RarityChancePets (chance each)
Common55%Moon Bunny, Astro Hamster (27.5% each)
Rare32%Comet Kitty, Star Owl, Space Axolotl (10.7% each)
Epic11%Robo Pug, Nebula Fox (5.5% each)
Legendary2%Galaxy Dragon (2%)

If you hatch 9 eggs in a row without an Epic or Legendary, the next one is guaranteed to be Epic or better.

An Epic+ Pet Egg (from the Starter Pack and the Star Pass) is always Epic or Legendary: Epic 84.6% (Robo Pug, Nebula Fox: 42.3% each) and Legendary 15.4% (Galaxy Dragon).

The odds are always shown in the game before you buy or hatch: Shop > Pet Egg > Odds, and the Odds links on the Starter Pack and the Star Pass.

The game drains my battery. What can I do?

Turn on Eco mode in Settings. It caps the game at 30 frames per second and shows fewer effects. Your earnings stay exactly the same.

Ads and privacy

Does the game have ads?

Only optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch:

  • ×2 Boost: +5 minutes of double damage and coins (stacks up to 30 minutes)
  • ×2 WATCH on offline earnings: doubles the coins
  • ×2 WATCH on the Free Gift: doubles the gift

There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. You only get the reward if you watch the video to the end.

"No ad available": why?

Ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available right away. Check your connection and try again in a minute. Everything else in the game works offline.

Why does the game ask to "track" me?

That's Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. It decides whether Google, our ad provider, may use your device's advertising identifier to show you more relevant ads. Either answer is fine, and the game plays exactly the same. You can change it any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and wherever else it applies), open Settings > Privacy options in the game. Our privacy policy explains what data the ads use.

Star Pass and purchases

What is the Star Pass?

A season of rewards that lasts 30 days. You earn Star Pass XP just by playing (defeating asteroids and bosses, rebirths, the Free Gift and offline earnings; no ads needed) and reach up to 30 tiers. Every tier has a free reward for everyone. The optional premium track adds a second reward per tier and 100 gems right away.

Rewards you reached but didn't claim are added automatically when the season ends. Premium is bought separately for each season and unlocks only that season. The season's dates are shown in the Star Pass.

Are there in-app purchases?

Yes, and they are optional: gem packs, a one-time Starter Pack and the Star Pass premium track. Everything else, gems included, can be earned by playing. Gems never expire and can't be exchanged for money. Prices are shown in your local currency on each button, and Apple confirms every purchase before you pay.

Purchases are not offered in Belgium. Everything else in the game works the same there.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap Restore purchases in Settings, at the bottom of Shop > GEMS, or in the Star Pass. This brings back the Starter Pack and any Star Pass seasons your Apple Account owns, for example after reinstalling or on a new iPhone. The game also checks this by itself when it starts.

Gems are used up when you spend them, so Apple doesn't restore them. They are saved on your iPhone with the rest of your progress, and deleting the app deletes them.

I bought something but it didn't arrive

Close the game completely and open it again with an internet connection: the game asks Apple for any purchase that hasn't been delivered yet. If a parent has to approve purchases (Ask to Buy), it arrives as soon as it is approved. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date and what you bought (your Apple receipt email helps).

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves because we never receive your payment.

How can I turn off or limit purchases?

On iPhone, open Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases > In-app Purchases and choose Don't Allow. With Family Sharing, a parent can turn on Ask to Buy for a child's account to approve each purchase.
