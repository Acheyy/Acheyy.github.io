Saving and progress

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Just open the game and play.

How is my progress saved?

Automatically, on your iPhone. The game saves every few seconds, after every purchase, hatch and boss win, and whenever you leave the app. Nothing is uploaded, so the game also works offline.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

There is no cloud save or account sync. Your progress is part of your iPhone's normal backup, so restoring an iCloud or computer backup, or transferring your data to a new iPhone during setup, usually brings it along. It does not sync between two devices.

I deleted the app. Can I get my progress back?

Unfortunately not. Deleting the app deletes its saved game, and we don't keep a copy. The only way back is an iPhone backup made before you deleted it.

How do I start over?

Open Settings (the gear button), tap Reset progress, and confirm twice ("Are you sure?", then "Tap again to wipe ALL"). This deletes everything (coins, stages, gems, pets, upgrades and settings) and can't be undone.

Playing

How do offline earnings work?

When you're away for at least a minute, your corgi keeps earning coins. You get up to 2 hours of earnings at 50% of your normal income. The Cozy Bed gem upgrade raises both the time limit and the rate, and the Star Owl pet adds to it. When you come back, tap COLLECT, or ×2 WATCH to double the coins with a short video ad.

Changing your iPhone's clock won't earn you more: big clock jumps are ignored.

What happens when a boss escapes?

The game keeps you farming the stage's normal waves so you can upgrade. Tap FIGHT BOSS when you're ready. With Auto boss on (Settings), the game retries the boss by itself after each farming loop.

What is Rebirth?

From stage 25 you can rebirth: you start again at stage 1, but you earn stars that permanently increase your damage and income (+10% each), plus gems. Your gems, pets, gem upgrades and stars are kept.

How do pet eggs work? What are the odds?

A Pet Egg costs 100 gems (or 10 eggs for 900 gems, with at least one Epic or better). Gems are earned only by playing: bosses, gold asteroids, rebirths and the Free Gift. You can't buy them with money.

Every egg hatches one of 8 pets: Common 55%, Rare 32%, Epic 11%, Legendary 2%. If you hatch 9 eggs in a row without an Epic or Legendary, the next one is guaranteed to be Epic or better. The odds are also shown in the game (Shop > Pet Egg > Odds).

The game drains my battery. What can I do?

Turn on Eco mode in Settings. It caps the game at 30 frames per second and shows fewer effects. Your earnings stay exactly the same.

Ads and privacy

Does the game have ads?

Only optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch:

  • ×2 Boost: +5 minutes of double damage and coins (stacks up to 30 minutes)
  • ×2 WATCH on offline earnings: doubles the coins
  • ×2 WATCH on the Free Gift: doubles the gift

There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. You only get the reward if you watch the video to the end.

"No ad available": why?

Ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available right away. Check your connection and try again in a minute. Everything else in the game works offline.

Why does the game ask to "track" me?

That's Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. It decides whether Google, our ad provider, may use your device's advertising identifier to show you more relevant ads. Either answer is fine, and the game plays exactly the same. You can change it any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and wherever else it applies), open Settings > Privacy options in the game. Our privacy policy explains what data the ads use.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. Corgi Blast has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment. Gems and everything else are earned by playing.
