Corgi Blast

Blast cute asteroids, beat giant bosses and hatch adorable space pets. Your corgi keeps earning while you sleep!

Coming soon to theApp Store Free on iPhone. No accounts and no in-app purchases.

What's inside

Tap and blast

Your corgi astronaut fires on its own. Tap the asteroids to add your own shots and land big golden crits.

Giant bosses

Every fifth wave a Crystal Golem or a Meteor King slams in. Beat it before the timer runs out to reach the next stage.

8 space pets

Hatch eggs with gems you earn by playing. Every pet boosts your corgi, and your Buddy flies along and fights beside you.

Upgrade and rebirth

Level up damage, fire rate and income, then rebirth for stars that make every run bigger than the last.

Earn while you're away

Close the game and your corgi keeps collecting coins for hours. Come back to a pile of loot.

Fair and free

No accounts and no purchases. Ads are optional: watch one only when you want a ×2 boost or a doubled reward.
